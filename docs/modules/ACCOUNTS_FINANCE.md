# Accounts & Finance (planned)

Must distinguish:

- Revenue/sales documents from cash receipts.
- Purchases from supplier payments.
- Customer receivables from supplier payables.
- Product cost/COGS from operating expenses.
- Gross profit from operating/net profit.
- Cash flow from accounting profit.

Candidate reports: expense by category, cash/bank movement, receivables aging, supplier balances, sales and purchase summaries, gross margin and profit reports. Definitions and journal mappings require accountant approval.
