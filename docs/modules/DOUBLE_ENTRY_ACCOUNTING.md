# Double-Entry Accounting (decision pending)

Full double-entry accounting adds complexity but offers a consistent ledger. Confirm with the shop owner and accountant whether it is needed in the first release.

Possible chart-of-account groups:
- Assets: cash, bank, receivables, inventory.
- Liabilities: supplier payables, taxes payable where applicable.
- Equity: owner capital/drawings.
- Revenue: sales and other income.
- Expenses: COGS, rent, utilities, wages and other expenses.

Illustrative templates only; accountant must approve exact treatment:
- Credit sale: debit receivable, credit sales; record COGS/inventory separately.
- Customer receipt: debit cash/bank, credit receivable.
- Credit purchase: debit inventory/appropriate cost, credit supplier payable.
- Supplier payment: debit supplier payable, credit cash/bank.
- Return: linked reversal/credit entries.

Every posted journal must balance. Do not represent these examples as tax or accounting advice.
