# Business Requirements

## Planned modules

1. Dashboard and business summaries.
2. Product catalogue and categories.
3. Inventory movement ledger, stock adjustments and low-stock warnings.
4. Supplier directory and supplier ledger.
5. Purchases, partial supplier payments and purchase returns.
6. Customer profiles, purchase history, payments and receivables.
7. POS sales, discounts, invoices and receipt printing.
8. Partial payments, credit sales and due collection.
9. Sales returns and refunds.
10. Expenses, cash/bank tracking and accounting reports.
11. Reports and analytics.
12. User accounts, permissions and audit logs.
13. Configuration, backup and recovery.
14. Optional public e-commerce module, subject to separate approval.

## Non-functional requirements

- Server-side authorization.
- Decimal-safe financial calculations.
- Transactional stock and payment operations.
- Traceable posted documents and corrections.
- Input validation and clear errors.
- Meaningful tests and auditable changes.
- Secure secret handling and tested backup/restore.

## Acceptance

Each phase must define its own concrete acceptance criteria and verification commands before implementation.
