# Glossary

- **POS:** Point of Sale; screen/process used to complete a shop sale.
- **SKU:** Internal product identifier.
- **Stock movement:** Recorded event that increases or decreases stock.
- **Receivable:** Amount a customer owes the shop.
- **Payable:** Amount the shop owes a supplier.
- **Partial payment:** Payment that settles only part of an invoice or balance.
- **Posted document:** Financial/inventory record finalized for normal business use.
- **Reversal:** Traceable correcting transaction linked to an earlier posted record.
- **COGS:** Cost of Goods Sold; cost assigned to products sold.
- **Weighted average costing:** Inventory costing method that averages eligible inventory costs by quantity.
- **RBAC:** Role-Based Access Control.
- **Audit log:** Record of who performed an important action and when.
